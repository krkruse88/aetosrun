# Aetos:run privacy policy

**Effective date:** November 24, 2026

Aetos:run ("the app") is an interval run timer for iPhone, made by Kevin Kruse ("we", "us", "I", "Me"). This policy explains what the app does with your information.

**The short version:** there are no accounts, no ads, and no tracking or analytics. We don't collect your data on our own servers, and we don't sell it. Your runs and settings stay on your iPhone unless you choose to back them up or share them.

## What stays on your iPhone

The app stores these on your device:

- Your interval workouts and saved workouts
- Your run history: date, duration, distance, route, place name, weather, your notes, and route pictures
- Your settings and your theme choice
- Your Pro purchase and free-trial status

We never receive this information.

## Location

- **During a run**, if GPS is on, the app uses your precise location, only while the app is in use, to measure distance and draw your route. This stays on your iPhone.
- **For weather**, the app uses your approximate location (accurate to about a kilometer). It sends those coordinates to [Open-Meteo](https://open-meteo.com), a weather service, to get the current temperature and conditions. See Open-Meteo's own privacy policy for how it handles requests. We don't see or store these requests.
- **For the place name** shown with a run, the app uses Apple's location services to turn coordinates into a town or neighborhood name.
- **For maps**, the street map and route pictures are provided by Apple Maps, so Apple receives map requests as it does for any app that shows Apple Maps. See Apple's privacy policy.

You can turn location off in iPhone Settings, or turn off **Weather from my location** and **Use GPS** in the app. The timer works without location.

## Apple Health (optional, Pro)

If you turn on **Save runs to Apple Health**, the app adds each finished run to the Health app as a running workout, with its distance and route. The app only writes to Health. It does not read your Health data. Your Health data is not shared with us or anyone else.

## Notifications and the Lock Screen timer

Alerts are local notifications created on your iPhone. The app doesn't use push notifications and doesn't send data to a server for them. The timer on the Lock Screen and Dynamic Island is also local.

## Purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple. We don't see or store your payment details. The app checks your purchase status with Apple so it can unlock Pro and restore purchases on your devices.

## Backups

To help you avoid losing your runs, the app keeps backups on your device:

- A backup file inside the app's own storage, visible in the Files app under "On My iPhone".
- A small backup copy of your run list in the iPhone's secure Keychain. This copy can survive deleting the app, so the app can offer to restore your runs if you reinstall it. It stays on your device and is removed when you erase the device.
- If you choose a **backup folder**, the app also saves backups there. If the folder is in iCloud Drive, Apple stores those files in your iCloud account under Apple's terms. We never receive them.

## Sharing

If you share a route picture or a workout link, it goes wherever you choose to send it. We don't receive it.

## Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from anyone.
Really kids should just run and play and be kids, get em off the screens. 

## Data deletion

You can delete runs in the app (they stay in Recently Deleted for 14 days). Deleting the app removes the data stored in it. You can delete backup files you saved yourself in the Files app. Because we don't collect your data, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the effective date above.
